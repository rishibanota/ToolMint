Privacy Policy

Overview

ToolMint is a collection of browser-side utility tools. Most tools process data entirely in your browser. We take your privacy seriously.

Data Processing

Your files, text, and data entered into our tools are processed locally in your browser. We do not upload, store, or transmit tool input/output data to our servers. Certain tools may use third-party libraries loaded from CDNs.

User Accounts & Authentication

We offer optional user accounts for ToolMint members. When you create an account, we store your email address and encrypted password. Account data is used solely for authentication and to provide unlimited tool access to signed-in users. You can delete your account at any time by contacting us.

Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Authentication cookies: To keep you signed in across browser sessions (set by our secure tm_auth cookie).
  • Usage tracking cookies: We use cookies (tm_usage_count and tm_usage_date) to track the number of tool uses per day for non-signed-in visitors. This enables our free tier limit of 5 uses per day. These cookies do not contain personal information.
  • Third-party cookies: Google AdSense / Adsterra (see Advertising section) may set cookies for ad personalization. You can manage ad preferences at Google's Ad Settings page.

Advertising

We use third-party advertising services to display advertisements on our site. These services may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting third-party ad settings.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand site usage patterns. We do not use intrusive tracking scripts. Any analytics we implement will be minimal and respect your privacy.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Secure Authentication Services for account verification
  • Cloudflare Workers for hosting
  • Adsterra for advertising
  • jsDelivr CDN for loading open-source libraries

Your Rights

You can clear your cookies at any time to reset your usage counter and remove authentication state. You can request deletion of your account data by contacting us at rishibanota837@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at rishibanota837@gmail.com.
