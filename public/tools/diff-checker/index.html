How to use

  1. Paste your original text into the input box.
  2. Paste your modified text into the second box.
  3. Click Compare Text to view line-by-line additions and deletions.

Frequently asked questions

How are line differences displayed?

Added lines are marked with (+), deleted lines with (-), and unchanged lines are shown normally.

Is there a limit on text length?

No artificial limits, all processing happens locally in your browser memory.
