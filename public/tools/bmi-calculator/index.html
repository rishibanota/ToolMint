How to use

  1. Select your preferred measurement unit (Metric or Imperial).
  2. Enter your height and weight values.
  3. Click Calculate BMI to view your BMI score and weight category.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI range?

A standard normal BMI for adults ranges between 18.5 and 24.9.

Does it support imperial units?

Yes, you can easily toggle between Metric (kg/cm) and Imperial (lbs/in).
