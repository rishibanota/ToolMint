Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest calculated on the initial principal as well as accumulated interest from previous periods.

How does compounding frequency affect returns?

Higher compounding frequency (e.g. monthly vs yearly) yields higher total accumulated interest.
