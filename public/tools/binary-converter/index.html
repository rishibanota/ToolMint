How to use

  1. Choose your conversion mode (Text to Binary or Binary to Text).
  2. Enter your input text or binary string.
  3. Click Convert to view the output.

Frequently asked questions

How are characters encoded?

Characters are converted into standard 8-bit UTF-8 binary bytes separated by spaces.

Can it convert binary numbers back to text?

Yes, select Binary to Text mode and paste space-separated binary bytes.
